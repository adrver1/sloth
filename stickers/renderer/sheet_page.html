Sloth babies stickers

13 stickers with the two baby sloths and the ponytail engineer, rendered from the same 3D models and clips as the game. Each one is a 512×512 WebP with a transparent background and a white outline, ready for WhatsApp. Tap a sticker to see it big.

Adding them to WhatsApp

  1. Open a sticker big and long-press it to save it to your photos. The zip of all 13 is also in the project files, sloth-stickers/sloth-stickers.zip.
  2. In any chat, open the sticker tray and tap Create, then pick the saved image. WhatsApp keeps the transparent background.
  3. For a whole pack in one go, a sticker maker app can import all 13 together. Use tray.png as the pack icon.
Captions are in English. Norwegian versions or captionless ones are a quick re-render.
