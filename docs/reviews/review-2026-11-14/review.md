# Canopy Camp review, second round (2026-11-14)

Reviewed branch `claude/project-thread-n6c974` at commit `b538272f7b7308f65ab75404f8e29a6e5deb095c` of adrver1/sloth on 2026-11-14. This merges ten review areas (five asset and generator areas, five code areas), each checked by a verifier, and a re-triage against the current code of the first review's ([code-review-2026-11-13](../code-review-2026-11-13/code-review.md)) open code findings and, in a later pass, its open generator findings; 6 old generator findings were not re-triaged on their own, because a new finding covers each (see Coverage gaps). The full records, with evidence, fixes and source ids, are in [findings.json](findings.json). Line numbers are on the reviewed commit; the branch head has since moved to 230b97f, which changes only tools/camp-scenarios.cjs.

## Criteria

Adriaen's criteria for this review: a finding matters by how much it improves the game for the player (bugs and glitches they can see, how the camp looks on a phone, smoothness and load time on a phone) or how much it speeds up development, including AI-driven iteration (honest checks, accurate comments, no stale numbers, fast and trustworthy bakes). Use as few triangles as still look good: spend them on silhouettes and things seen up close, paint detail into colours or textures, and never let tools add hidden detail (caps nobody sees, subdivision for bakes, high segment counts on small parts); state triangle counts per object for every exported model. Keep every asset variant (the plush, baby and plush_real sloth styles, and the flat sticker set). Anything that changes how something looks or how the game behaves is Adriaen's pick, and removals or retiring options are his decision on scope. Fixes that need a GPU light rebake are batched so one rebake covers them.

KTX2/UASTC quality was not checked (toktx is not installed). Light-map atlas texels and seams were not checked.
- Audio: nothing was heard, because a cloud session cannot. The audio findings (for example R2-011 and R2-056) cover logic only, and Adriaen checks the mix by ear. The boot area made no browser runs and read only about 300 of audio.js's 498 lines.
- glade.js and deadwood.js were read only in part (world-27 was not re-checked in depth). atmosphere.js pools and lantern geometry were only partly covered. tools/camp-scenarios.cjs was read only at its head.
- The camp's look in rain and at dusk, the jeep interior beyond one door close-up, food items while carried, and the sloth and operator in sloth-eye or future camera modes.

- The sloth style variants plush and plush_real (the keep-every-variant rule), and the archived build_camp styles. The generators area explicitly skipped the archived styles.
- tools/tours/*.json (28 scenario and tour files): their contents and correctness were not reviewed.
- The other apps: karaoke, synth, stickers-page, playtime, catch-the-baby, fur-options and playtime-tools, except for the files they share with the camp. The stickers renderer (stickers/renderer/index.html, sheet_page.html, stk_run.cjs, stk_anim.cjs) and the sticker packs were not reviewed, including the 500 KB WhatsApp limit and the no-clipped-heads rule.
- characters/aussie_critters (build_critters.py, critter_rig.py).
- The canopy prototype pages other than camp.html (index.html, nooks.html, names-prototype.html) were looked at only as deploy and hygiene clutter, not as pages.

- The jeep hidden-face ray pass (R2-148) was not re-run by its verifier, only spot-checked.
- Evidence files (PNGs, scripts, measurements) live in the session scratchpad named in each finding's evidence_dir. A reboot or cleanup can clear it, so copy what matters before relying on it.
