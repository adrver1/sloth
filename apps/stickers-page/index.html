Sloth babies stickers

19 stickers with the baby sloths, the ponytail engineer and the bearded operator, including five with hearts (14 to 18) and a happy family swing (19), in fuzzy fur, rendered from the same 3D models and clips as the game. Each comes as an animated sticker (a 2 to 4 second loop of its clip) and a still one. All are 512×512 WebP, already cut out with a white die-cut border, and every frame is framed so nothing is clipped. Tap one to see it big.

Adding them to WhatsApp

  1. WhatsApp keeps a pack all animated or all still, so pick one set. Download the zip from the sticker thread in the project (sloth-stickers-animated.zip with all 19, sloth-hearts.zip for the 5 hearts, or sloth-stickers.zip for stills), or open a sticker here big and long-press to save it.
  2. Animated: WhatsApp can't import sticker files by itself, so it takes a free sticker app. Unzip the download (tap it in the Files app on iPhone, or use Files by Google on Android). In Sticker.ly (iPhone and Android) or Personal Stickers for WhatsApp (Android, imports WebP as is), make a new animated pack, add at least 3 of the WebP files, pick tray.png as the icon, and tap Add to WhatsApp. Each sticker is under 500 KB and loops in under 10 seconds, as WhatsApp requires.
  3. Still: open a sticker big, long-press to save it, then in a chat tap Create in the sticker tray and pick it. It is already cut out, so skip background removal.
Captions are in English. Norwegian or captionless versions are a quick re-render.
