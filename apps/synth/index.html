Sloth Synth

A modular rack for slow, dreamy atmospheres. Patched by the baby sloth from the band.

Patch: drag from one jack to another, or tap one jack and then the other. Unplug: grab the plug on an input and drop it anywhere off a jack. Knobs: drag up or down, double-tap to reset. Every knob has a small jack under it: plug a CV or an oscillator in and it turns the knob for you.

Audio, a sound CV, a slow control voltage Trigger, a tap (heart jacks) Audio and CV jacks plug into each other freely; triggers only into triggers.
